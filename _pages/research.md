### Job Market Paper <a name="JMP"></a>

- [**Signed, Sealed, Delivered: Digital Receipts in the Ugandan Dairy Chain**](https://github.com/pmasae/pmasae.github.io/blob/changes/_publications/PFMS_JMP.pdf) — with [David Henning](https://djhenning.github.io/)
  - *Presented at:* AFE 2025, AAEA & WAEA 2025
  - [AEA RCT Registry](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/14087)
	
	**Abstract:** 
	We provide causal evidence that SMS-based digital receipts–messages reporting daily milk deliveries–improve accountability, delivery behavior, and product quality in agricultural markets with weak monitoring. By turning imperfect transaction information into verifiable digital records, receipts enhance transparency and make intermediary behavior more observable. In a randomized experiment with dairy cooperatives in western Uganda, the intervention increased milk quality and, among farmers relying on intermediaries to transport the product, increased discrepancy detection and switching from dishonest intermediaries. Self-deliverers, facing low information frictions, delivered more frequently without changing average volume. The intervention effects vary with the intensity of information frictions. In high-friction settings, among farmers who use transporters, receipts imposes discipline on intermediaries. In low-friction settings, where individuals self-deliver, receipts nudge farmers to deliver milk more frequently. Overall, we provide evidence that simple digital tools can reduce information asymmetry and strengthen accountability in smallholder supply chains.
