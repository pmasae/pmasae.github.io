### Selected research in progress <a name="in-progress"></a>

- **Signed, Sealed, Delivered: Digital Receipts in the Ugandan Dairy Chain** - with [David Henning](https://djhenning.github.io/) (Data collection completed, Draft in progress) 
	
	[AEA RCT Registry](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/14087)
	
	**Abstract:** We provide causal evidence on whether digital receipts —SMS messages reporting milk deliveries— can improve accountability, farmer participation, and product quality in agricultural markets. In a randomized experiment with dairy cooperatives in western Uganda, we find that treated farmers —particularly those who self-deliver— are significantly more likely to deliver milk. This increase in delivery activity occurs despite no change in total volumes, suggesting a shift along the extensive margin. The intervention also improves milk quality: treated farmers deliver milk with lower water content, as measured by lactometer readings. Among those who rely on transporters, digital receipts increase the likelihood of detecting discrepancies, switching baseline transporters, and reporting lower trust in intermediaries. By turning unobservable transactions into timely, verifiable records, digital receipts enhance transparency and observability, helping to realign incentives along the supply chain. These findings show how simple digital tools can reduce information frictions, encourage market participation, and improve product quality in fragmented agricultural systems.
