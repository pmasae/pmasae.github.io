### Selected research in progress <a name="in-progress"></a>

- **Signed, Sealed, Delivered: Digital Receipts in the Ugandan Dairy Chain** - with [David Henning](https://djhenning.github.io/) (Data collection completed, Draft in progress) 
	
	[AEA RCT Registry](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/14087)
	
	**Abstract:**We provide causal evidence on whether digital receipts—SMS messages reporting milk deliveries—can improve accountability and product quality in agricultural markets. In a randomized experiment with dairy cooperatives in western Uganda, we find that digital receipts reduce milk dilution: treated farmers deliver significantly higher-quality milk, as measured by lactometer readings. While total volumes remain unchanged, treated farmers—particularly smallholders—are more likely to deliver during the rainy season. Among those using transporters, the intervention increases the likelihood of detecting discrepancies, switching baseline transporters, and reporting lower trust in intermediaries. By turning unobservable transactions into timely, verifiable records, the digital receipts enhance transparency and observability, helping to realign incentives along the supply chain. These findings show how simple digital tools can reduce information frictions, strengthen contract enforcement, improve product quality, and empower farmers in fragmented agricultural markets.
